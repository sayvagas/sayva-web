Prize Rules: Weekly Draw and Monthly Leaderboard

Last updated: {publication date}

These official rules ("Rules") govern the SAYVA weekly gift card draw (the "Weekly Draw") and the SAYVA monthly leaderboard prizes (the "Monthly Leaderboard"), together the "Promotion", offered in the SAYVA mobile application ("App"). By taking part you agree to these Rules and to the SAYVA Terms of Service. If these Rules and the Terms of Service conflict, these Rules apply to the Promotion.

1. Sponsor

The Promotion is sponsored by {sponsor legal name, e.g. 17669186 Canada Inc.}, {sponsor mailing address} ("SAYVA", "we", "us" or "our"). The Promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Apple, Google or any gift card brand.

2. No Purchase Necessary

No purchase is necessary to enter or win. Entries are earned by submitting gas price photos in the App. Extra entries can also be obtained with points, but points are earned free in the App and can't be purchased with money. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning.

3. Where and When the Promotion Runs

  • Participating cities. The Promotion runs only in the cities listed here, from the start date shown: {participating cities and start dates}. A city's prizes appear in the App only once its Promotion has started.
  • Draw weeks. Each Weekly Draw covers one week, from Monday at 00:00 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) to the following Monday at 00:00 UTC. In Eastern time a week closes on Sunday at 8:00 PM (7:00 PM when Eastern Standard Time applies). Entries do not carry over from one week to the next.
  • Leaderboard months. Each Monthly Leaderboard covers one calendar month in UTC, from the 1st at 00:00 UTC to the 1st of the next month at 00:00 UTC.
  • End of the Promotion. The Promotion runs until {end date of the beta Promotion}, unless it is ended earlier under section 14.

4. Who Can Take Part

The Promotion is open to legal residents of {contest region, e.g. Canada excluding Quebec} who are at least {minimum age, e.g. the age of majority in their province or territory} and who have a SAYVA account. To be eligible for a prize, when the draw or ranking runs:

  • your account must be at least 14 days old at the end of the week or month;
  • you must have at least 10 approved photos (accepted price submissions, in any city) by the end of the week or month;
  • your account must be in good standing: not suspended, not deleted, and not a re-registration of a previously deleted account.

The age and photo thresholds above are the current settings. The App shows your progress toward them. Employees of SAYVA {and of its agencies, and members of their households} are not eligible to win. {Exclusions to be confirmed by counsel.}

You can collect entries before you are eligible, but entries held by an ineligible account are left out of the draw entirely.

5. How to Get Entries

Photo entries (free)

  • Each approved photo (a gas price photo accepted by SAYVA) gives you 1 entry in that week's Weekly Draw for the city of the station in the photo.
  • You can get up to 30 photo entries per day, counted per UTC day across all cities. Photos above that limit are still accepted but give no entry.
  • An entry is given when the photo is approved, and it counts in the week in which it was approved.

Extra entries obtained with points

  • Extra entries can be obtained with points in the SAYVA shop in the App, up to {30} per person per calendar month (UTC), which is the current setting. The number of points needed per entry and the maximum per transaction are shown in the shop.
  • Points are earned free in the App, for example for approved photos, the daily spin, challenges and referrals. Points can't be purchased with money and have no cash value.
  • Extra entries are only available once you are eligible (section 4) and while the Promotion is running in your home city. They count in your home city's Weekly Draw for the current week, in the same way as photo entries.
  • Extra entries never affect the Monthly Leaderboard, which counts approved photos only, or your eligibility.
  • SAYVA may cancel extra entries obtained in error or in breach of these Rules before that week's draw runs; the points used are then returned.

No other method of entry is available. Entries cannot be transferred, sold or combined between accounts.

6. The Weekly Draw

  • A Weekly Draw may cover a single participating city or a group of participating cities. When cities are grouped, entries earned in all of those cities go into one shared draw for one set of prizes. {Draw areas: the cities in each Weekly Draw.}
  • The draw runs automatically after the week closes, on Monday at about 06:00 UTC (2:00 AM Eastern, or 1:00 AM when Eastern Standard Time applies).
  • Winners are selected at random from eligible entrants, weighted by entries: each entry is one chance. The selection uses a recorded random seed so that every draw can be independently re-run and checked.
  • Each person can win at most one prize per draw. Once selected, a winner is removed before the next prize is drawn.

7. The Monthly Leaderboard

  • Each participating city has its own Monthly Leaderboard. Eligible users are ranked by the number of approved photos at stations in that city during the month. Repeat photos at the same station count.
  • Ties are broken by the points earned that month (more points ranks higher), and then by a fixed technical order.
  • Points, extra entries and other activity never place you on the leaderboard by themselves: you need at least one approved photo in the month.
  • The top {3} eligible users win the monthly prizes. The ranking is finalized after the month ends, on the 1st at about 06:00 UTC. If a user is not eligible, the prize passes to the next eligible user.

Prizes are digital gift cards in Canadian dollars. Depending on how prizes are delivered at the time, a winner either chooses a card from a list of brands offered in the App or receives a gift card chosen by SAYVA by email. Weekly Draw prizes are set per draw (a city or a group of cities); Monthly Leaderboard prizes are set per city:

  • Weekly Draw: {weekly prizes per draw, e.g. 1 × $50 and 5 × $10 gift cards}.
  • Monthly Leaderboard: {monthly prizes per city, e.g. $75, $50 and $25 gift cards for 1st, 2nd and 3rd}.

The approximate retail value of all prizes in each city is {total prize value per draw per week, and per city per month}. Prizes must be accepted as awarded and cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred, except that SAYVA may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Gift cards are subject to their issuers' terms. A gift card offered as a choice of brands must be chosen within {90} days of being sent, after which it expires. {Expiry to be confirmed by counsel for each province.}

{Odds statement, e.g. The odds of winning a Weekly Draw prize depend on the number of eligible entries received in that draw for that week, across every city the draw covers.} The App shows your own entries for the current week.

10. Winners, Skill-Testing Question and Claiming

  • Selected winners are notified in the App (and by push notification when notifications are on).
  • Before being declared a winner, a selected entrant must correctly answer, without assistance, a mathematical skill-testing question in the App. Three attempts are allowed.
  • The prize must be claimed in the App within 14 days of the draw or ranking. A prize not claimed in time, or for which the skill-testing question is not answered correctly, is forfeited.
  • Where the App asks for an email address to deliver the prize, the winner must provide a valid one.

11. Review and Verification

A SAYVA team member reviews every prize before it is sent. SAYVA may verify eligibility and compliance with these Rules and the Terms of Service, and may cancel a prize or disqualify an entrant for fraud, multiple accounts, false or manipulated price submissions, automated activity or any other breach. A cancelled prize is not redrawn. {Confirm with counsel: redraw or forfeit.} Eligibility is checked using account age and approved photos as of the end of the week or month, and account standing when the draw or ranking runs.

The App may show recent winners in your city. A winner's username is shown only if the winner has opted in; otherwise the winner is shown as "A Sayva member". {Publicity consent wording to be confirmed by counsel.}
