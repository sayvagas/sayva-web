Prize Rules: Weekly Draw, Monthly Leaderboard and Boxes

Last updated: {publication date}

These official rules ("Rules") govern the SAYVA weekly gift card draw (the "Weekly Draw"), the SAYVA monthly leaderboard prizes (the "Monthly Leaderboard") and the boxes offered in the SAYVA shop (the "Boxes", section 8), together the "Promotion", offered in the SAYVA mobile application ("App"). By taking part you agree to these Rules and to the SAYVA Terms of Service. If these Rules and the Terms of Service conflict, these Rules apply to the Promotion.

1. Sponsor

The Promotion is sponsored by {sponsor legal name, e.g. 17669186 Canada Inc.}, {sponsor mailing address} ("SAYVA", "we", "us" or "our"). The Promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Apple, Google or any gift card brand.

2. No Purchase Necessary

No purchase is necessary to enter or win. Entries are earned by submitting gas price photos in the App. Extra entries and Boxes can also be obtained with points, but only with points: points are earned free in the App and can't be purchased with money. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning.

3. Where and When the Promotion Runs

  • Participating cities. The Promotion runs only in the cities listed here, from the start date shown: {participating cities and start dates}. A city's prizes appear in the App only once its Promotion has started.
  • Draw weeks. Each Weekly Draw has a draw time: one day of the week and a local time, set for that draw and shown in the App (section 6). A draw week runs from one draw time to the next, and the next week starts at that same moment. Entries do not carry over from one week to the next. If SAYVA changes a draw's prizes or draw time, the change applies from the following week; the week in progress keeps the prizes and draw time shown when it started. The week in which a new draw time first applies may be shorter or longer than seven days, but never shorter than three and a half days.
  • Leaderboard months. Each Monthly Leaderboard covers one calendar month in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), from the 1st at 00:00 UTC until the end of the last day of the month at 11:59 PM UTC (7:59 PM Eastern Daylight Time, or 6:59 PM when Eastern Standard Time applies).
  • End of the Promotion. The Promotion runs until {end date of the beta Promotion}, unless it is ended earlier under section 15.

4. Who Can Take Part

The Promotion is open to legal residents of {contest region, e.g. Canada excluding Quebec} who are at least {minimum age, e.g. the age of majority in their province or territory} and who have a SAYVA account. You can take part from the moment you create your account: there is no minimum account age and no minimum number of photos. To be eligible for a prize, your account must be in good standing when the draw or ranking runs, or when a Box is opened: not suspended, not deleted, and not a re-registration of a previously deleted account.

Employees of SAYVA {and of its agencies, and members of their households} are not eligible to win. {Exclusions to be confirmed by counsel.}

Entries held by an account that is not in good standing are left out of the draw entirely.

5. How to Get Entries

Photo entries (free)

  • Each approved photo (a gas price photo accepted by SAYVA) gives you 1 entry in that week's Weekly Draw that covers the city of the station in the photo.
  • You can get up to 30 photo entries per day, counted per UTC day across all cities. Photos above that limit are still accepted but give no entry.
  • An entry is given when the photo is approved, and it counts in the draw week that is open at the moment of approval. A photo submitted before a draw time but approved after it counts in the following week's Weekly Draw.

Extra entries obtained with points

  • Extra entries can be obtained with points in the SAYVA shop in the App, up to {30} per person per calendar month (UTC), which is the current setting. The number of points needed per entry and the maximum per transaction are shown in the shop.
  • Points are earned free in the App, for example for approved photos, the daily spin, challenges and referrals. Points can't be purchased with money and have no cash value.
  • Extra entries are available from the moment you have an account in good standing (section 4), while the Promotion is running in your home city. They count in the current week's Weekly Draw that covers your home city, in the same way as photo entries.
  • Extra entries never affect the Monthly Leaderboard, which counts approved photos only, or your eligibility.
  • SAYVA may cancel extra entries obtained in error or in breach of these Rules before that week's draw runs; the points used are then returned.

Entries won in a Box

  • Entries can also be won in a Box (section 8). They count in the current week's Weekly Draw that covers your home city, in the same way as extra entries obtained with points, and never affect the Monthly Leaderboard.

Golden station entries

  • Each day, SAYVA picks one gas station at random in each city covered by a Weekly Draw as that day's golden station, and shows it in the App. The day is the local day in that draw's time zone.
  • Your first approved photo of the golden station, taken on the day it is the golden station, gives you {10} bonus entries (the current setting, shown in the App), in addition to the photo entry. You can get this bonus once per golden station per day.
  • Bonus entries are given when the photo is approved and count in the draw week that is open at that moment, in the Weekly Draw that covers the city of the station, in the same way as photo entries. They do not count toward the daily limit on photo entries and never affect the Monthly Leaderboard.

No other method of entry is available. Entries cannot be transferred, sold or combined between accounts.

6. The Weekly Draw

  • A Weekly Draw may cover a single participating city or a group of participating cities. When cities are grouped, entries earned in all of those cities go into one shared draw for one set of prizes. {Draw areas: the cities in each Weekly Draw.}
  • Each Weekly Draw has its own draw time, shown in the App: {Draw day and time for each Weekly Draw, e.g. Fridays at 12:00 PM Eastern Time}.
  • At the draw time, entries for that week close and the winners are selected, at the same moment. The draw runs automatically, and winners are notified in the App right away, normally within a few minutes.
  • Only entries given before the draw time count in that week's draw. A photo entry is given when the photo is approved, not when it is submitted (section 5).
  • Winners are selected at random from eligible entrants, weighted by entries: each entry is one chance. The selection uses a recorded random seed so that every draw can be independently re-run and checked.
  • Each person can win at most one prize per draw. Once selected, a winner is removed before the next prize is drawn.

7. The Monthly Leaderboard

  • Each participating city has its own Monthly Leaderboard. Eligible users are ranked by the number of approved photos at stations in that city during the month. Repeat photos at the same station count.
  • Ties are broken by the points earned that month (more points ranks higher), and then by a fixed technical order.
  • Points, extra entries and other activity never place you on the leaderboard by themselves: you need at least one approved photo in the month.
  • The top {3} eligible users win the monthly prizes. The ranking closes and is finalized at the end of each calendar month in UTC: at 11:59 PM UTC on the last day of the month (7:59 PM Eastern Daylight Time, or 6:59 PM when Eastern Standard Time applies). Winners are notified in the App right away. If a user is not eligible, the prize passes to the next eligible user.

8. Boxes

  • The SAYVA shop in the App offers Boxes {e.g. the Regular, Plus and Premium boxes}, obtained with points only. The points needed to open each Box are shown in the shop. Points can't be purchased with money.
  • Each Box contains entries, points or gift cards. What a Box contains is shown on that Box in the shop before you open it.
  • Opening a Box gives exactly one of its prizes, selected at random by SAYVA's server when the Box is opened, and shown in the App right away. Every opening is recorded so that it can be checked.
  • Entries won in a Box count in the Weekly Draw as described in section 5. A Box that can contain entries is only available while the Weekly Draw runs in your home city.
  • Points won in a Box are added to your balance. They are not earned points: they never count toward the Monthly Leaderboard or its tie-breaks.
  • A gift card won in a Box is claimed in the App with the skill-testing question (section 11) and is reviewed by a SAYVA team member before it is sent (section 12).
  • Prizes are subject to monthly availability. Gift cards are available in limited numbers each calendar month (UTC). While a gift card is not available, it can't be won from a Box, and the Box gives one of its other prizes. {Availability wording to be confirmed by counsel.}
  • Boxes and their prizes can't be exchanged for cash, transferred or refunded, except that SAYVA may reverse an opening made in error or in breach of these Rules: the points used are returned and the prize is taken back.

Gift cards obtained with points

The shop also offers gift cards obtained directly with points, in limited numbers each calendar month (UTC). These are a redemption of points, not a prize: nothing is left to chance, no skill-testing question applies, and they are not part of the Promotion. Each one is reviewed by a SAYVA team member before it is sent and is subject to its issuer's terms.

11. Winners, Skill-Testing Question and Claiming

  • Selected winners are notified in the App (and by push notification when notifications are on).
  • Before being declared a winner, a selected entrant must correctly answer, without assistance, a mathematical skill-testing question in the App. Three attempts are allowed.
  • The prize must be claimed in the App within 14 days of the draw or ranking. A prize not claimed in time, or for which the skill-testing question is not answered correctly, is forfeited.
  • A gift card won in a Box has no claim deadline, but the skill-testing question applies in the same way. A gift card for which it is not answered correctly is forfeited.
  • Where the App asks for an email address to deliver the prize, the winner must provide a valid one.

12. Review and Verification

A SAYVA team member reviews every prize before it is sent. SAYVA may verify eligibility and compliance with these Rules and the Terms of Service, and may cancel a prize or disqualify an entrant for fraud, multiple accounts, false or manipulated price submissions, automated activity or any other breach. A cancelled prize is not redrawn. {Confirm with counsel: redraw or forfeit.} Eligibility is checked using account standing when the draw or ranking runs, or when a Box is opened.
